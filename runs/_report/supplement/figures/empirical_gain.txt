Empirical leader-to-follower speed gain of the drivers against the laws (D131). Drivers: per event the H1 estimate |P_xy| / P_xx of Welch's method (Hann segments, 50 % overlap, linear detrending); median (black) and 25-75 % range (grey) over the events of a speed bin (the event's mean follower speed; bins with at least 20 events, their number in the panel), at the Welch lines in 0.3-2 rad/s; dotted: the pooled H1 estimate of the bin (the spectra of all its segments averaged before the ratio; a sensitivity, not the statistic of D131). Laws: the numerical gains of the audits of the E1 runs (follownet_highd, folds 0-4, seed 0), the log gain interpolated to the cell (the median speed of its events) and averaged over the folds; dashed: IDM and k-NN. NGSIM I-80: events of at least 40 s, 40-s segments; HighD (a deviation of D131): its 15-s events, one segment each. NGSIM I-80: 3592 events; HighD (deviation: 14.9-s segments): 12512 events.
