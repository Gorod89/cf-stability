# Two-timescale certified hybrid with a persistent latent: pilot and choice of τ_z (D135)

- Pilot of D135: residual_idm_tt2 (the latent carried along the vehicle, z in [-0.2, 1]) on follownet_highd, fold 0, seed 0, tau_z 15, 30 and 60 s (configs/queue/e4_tt2_pilot.yaml). val/test RMSE s: closed-loop spacing RMSE, mean over the events (m), the latent from z = 0 at the first window of every event. admissible budget: the largest r_max x lipschitz of the a priori certificate over the 21 frozen latents of [-0.2, 1], lipschitz = 0.9 x it / r_max.
- unstable eq. z=..: share of the unstable equilibria (numerical rule, gain > 1.02) among the equilibria in the support of the speeds at the frozen latent (scripts/audit_stability.py frozen_z=<z>; the five z of D129, and z = -0.2, the lower end of the range, for information); band stable: the band share "stable inside the band" (D79). online: the audit of the persistent model (stability.json: the equilibria of the latent at its steady state, the frequency-response rollouts from it, the latent carried). certificate: the number of the 21 frozen latents of [-0.2, 1] at which the scanned a priori certificate holds at every grid speed (certificate.json).
- z: the persistent latent of the teacher-forced pass along the observed test events (from z = 0 before the first window, once per window), at every 10th window end; event ends: at the last window of every event; bound in an event: (1 - (1 - dt / tau_z)^n) x 1 with n = 121 windows (the 15-s events of highD): the latent cannot go further within one event; 0.182: the bound of the window latent of D129 at tau_z = 15 s.
- Choice (rule of D129 with the online audit of H-N2'): the smallest validation RMSE among the runs with no unstable equilibrium at any frozen z of D129 and in the online audit: tau_z = 15 s.
