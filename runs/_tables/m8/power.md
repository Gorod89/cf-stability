# Seed-to-seed spread and power of the corridor comparisons (D122)

- Power of the corridor design (D122): idm_global and residual_idm_certified, unit run (scenario and seed), paired by seed within every scenario (pairs).
- SD: standard deviation over the seeds within a scenario, pooled over the scenarios of the corridor (sqrt of the mean within-scenario variance); SD of the difference: the same for the paired difference residual_idm_certified - idm_global.
- Seeds needed to detect a difference of 10 % of the mean of the two laws (10 % of the mean) at alpha 0.05 (two-sided) with power 0.8: paired t-test (noncentral t) with the observed SD of the difference; one scenario: the seeds of a single scenario; all scenarios: seeds per scenario when every scenario of the corridor contributes its pairs (the scenario means removed, df = scenarios x (seeds - 1)).
- power of the design: of that test with the seeds of the design on every scenario; detectable: the smallest difference (relative to the mean) the design detects with power 0.8; observed: mean(residual_idm_certified) / mean(idm_global) - 1 over the pairs with its interval.
- Intervals: 95 % percentile bootstrap, 1000 resamples, seed 0.
