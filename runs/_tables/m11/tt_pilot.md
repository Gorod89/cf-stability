# Two-timescale certified hybrid: pilot and choice of τ_z (D129)

- Pilot of D129: residual_idm_tt on follownet_highd, fold 0, seed 0, tau_z 15, 30 and 60 s (configs/queue/e4_tt_pilot.yaml). val/test RMSE s: closed-loop spacing RMSE, mean over the events of the part (m). reach of z: 1 - (1 - dt / tau_z)^30, the largest |z| of the online latent (z_0 = 0 at the start of the window of 30 states); admissible budget: the largest r_max x lipschitz of the a priori certificate over the frozen latents of the reachable range (21 values), lipschitz = 0.9 x it / r_max.
- unstable eq. z=..: share of the unstable equilibria (numerical rule, gain > 1.02) among the equilibria in the support of the speeds at the frozen latent (scripts/audit_stability.py frozen_z=<z>); band stable: the band share "stable inside the band" (D79; the rest: equilibria outside the band). stable at every frozen z: no unstable equilibrium at any of the five. online: the audit of the model as it runs (stability.json). certificate: the number of the 21 frozen latents -1, -0.9, ..., 1 at which the scanned a priori certificate holds at every grid speed, and whether it holds on the reachable range (certificate.json).
- Choice (D129): the smallest validation RMSE among the runs with 100 % stable equilibria at every frozen z: tau_z = 15 s.
