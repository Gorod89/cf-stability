# Predictive validity of single-vehicle metrics for the macro error (D127, H-N1)

- H-N1 (D127): Spearman correlation of the macro error of a (law, corridor) pair (mean over its runs, runs/_tables/m5/laws.csv) with single-vehicle predictors of the law; per corridor over its laws, pooled with the corridor as a stratum (ranks within the corridor scaled to (0, 1), Pearson of the pooled ranks). pairs: the 37 pairs of correlation_pooled (19 laws on I-80, 18 on US-101), and the separate check with the six pairs of the ablation laws (idm_core_margin, idm_margin_i80, residual_idm_margin_free_r0.3) added (43).
- rho pooled with the 95 % percentile interval of a cluster bootstrap over the laws (5000 resamples; the rows of a law on both corridors move together; cf_stability.eval.stats.cluster_bootstrap_spearman); p: two-sided permutation test (10000 permutations): the instability of whole laws permuted (linked_permutation_p) for the predictors (a)-(e), one value per law; within every corridor (stratified_permutation_p) for (f)-(h), one value per pair.
- rho - rho(a): the difference of the pooled Spearman correlations of P and of the pre-specified predictor (a) over the pairs where both exist, with the percentile interval of the same law resamples for both (paired cluster bootstrap, numpy default_rng(20261019): 5000 draws of the laws, the same for every predictor).
- E: residual_idm_certified; D: residual_idm_margin_free_r0.3; their values of P on each corridor (the per-law predictors have one value on both). D vs E: P ranks E better than D when P(E) < P(D) strictly on both corridors (every predictor is oriented so that a larger value means a larger expected error, except (h), the raw index, shown as the contract names it, and (h') its distance to the drivers).
- rule (D127): P predicts better when the interval of rho - rho(a) lies above 0 and P ranks E better than D on both corridors; H-N1 is confirmed when the frozen-gain statistic (f) predicts better, partially confirmed when another predictor does, refuted when none does (the verdict is the main session's).
- (e): the transient growth of the braking-pulse platoon, max over the 50 followers of the speed standard deviation over that of follower 1; a collided platoon counts as +inf (inf in the table; ranked above every finite value); mean over the members (inf once a member collided).
- check: (b) of the member audits equals not_stable of runs/_tables/m5/instability.csv for 26 pairs (largest difference 1.1e-16).
- check: (a) recomputed in closed form for the laws without member runs equals the tables for 9 pairs (largest difference 7.6e-03).
- statistics recomputed from runs/_tables/m11/predictive_validity_pairs.csv (the predictor values of the run that wrote it).
