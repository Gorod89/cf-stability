# Materials of the manuscript

Written by `python scripts/paper_assets.py --manifest`; do not edit by hand.

The manuscript (*String Stability of Learned Car-Following Models: Audit, Differentiable Penalties, a Certified
Hybrid and Corridor-Level Validation*, submitted to *Mathematics*, MDPI, 2026) is not part of this repository. Its
generated tables and figure environments are written by `python scripts/paper_assets.py --out <dir>` (default
`paper/v2`, the authors' copy of the manuscript; `--out paper_assets` in a checkout). One row per table and figure,
numbered as in the manuscript: its label, the generated files (relative to `<dir>`), the files of this repository
it is built from (every CSV and report its builder reads; a figure is copied unchanged from its PDF) and the
commands that write them, upstream first. Tables 1, 2, 3 and 7 of the main text are written by hand in the
manuscript.

## Commands

The section Reproduce of `README.md` gives the inputs of every command and the three levels of reproduction.

| command | writes |
|---|---|
| `python scripts/analysis/full_history_audit.py` | the table `full_history` of `runs/_tables/m9/` and Figure S22 (`full_history_gain` of `runs/_report/supplement/figures/`); rewrites the `full_history.json` of the recurrent runs, which make_tables.py reads |
| `python scripts/analysis/threshold_sensitivity.py` | the table `threshold_sensitivity` of `runs/_tables/m9/` |
| `python scripts/analysis/equilibrium_roots.py` | the table `equilibrium_roots` of `runs/_tables/m9/` |
| `python scripts/analysis/certificate_exact.py` | the table `certificate_exact` of `runs/_tables/m9/` |
| `python scripts/analysis/band_sensitivity.py` | the table `band_sensitivity` of `runs/_tables/m8/` (needs the training events of level 3) |
| `python scripts/make_tables.py` | `runs/_tables/m4/` (E1-E5, the arms of E2, the verdicts of H1) and the table `e2_monotone` of `runs/_tables/m8/` |
| `python scripts/corridor_metrics.py compute=false tables=true` | `runs/_tables/m5/` (laws, components, instability, TOST, H12, e4_rmax, sensitivity) and the corridor tables of `runs/_tables/m8/` (asymmetry, asymmetry_contrasts, correlation_pooled, correlation_pooled_laws, power, temporal, correlation_clustered, contacts_absolute, h12_2_error, ablation_pairs) from the `macro.json` and `asymmetry.json` of every corridor run and ground truth (`asymmetry.json`: `python scripts/corridor_asymmetry.py`, level 3) |
| `python scripts/make_figures_supplement.py` | the tables `lowfreq_expansion`, `certificate_tightness` and `band_width` of `runs/_tables/m8/` and the supplementary figures except S22 (`runs/_report/supplement/figures/`); band_width needs the event sets of level 3, the corridor grids S10-S21 the `trajectories.npz` (level 3), else the `fields.npz` of the corridor runs |
| `python scripts/make_report.py` | `runs/_report/tables/` (the tables above, copied, and the tables derived from them), the seven figures of `runs/_report/figures/` (Figures 5 and 6 from the `trajectories.npz`, else the `fields.npz` of the corridor runs) and `runs/_report/report.md` |
| `python scripts/paper_assets.py --out <dir>` | the generated files of the tables below |
